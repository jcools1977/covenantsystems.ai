The Principles

How the work
gets done.

Kingdom first

This work is a calling before it is a business. I build for the Kingdom first, which shows up as integrity in the build, honesty in the room, and technology put in service of the people it touches.

BB4C

Breath before code

Pause to understand the problem before writing the solution. The best system is the one built on the right diagnosis, not the fastest demo.

TWB

Trust. Walk. Build.

In that order. Trust before the plan, walk before the sprint, build only what the first two have earned. It is how covenants work, and how good systems get made.

You own your data

Your data is yours, fully portable, and never held hostage. I build on systems you control, so you are never locked into a tool you cannot walk away from.

Intentions matter

A tool is only as good as the intentions behind it. I build for operators who want to do right by their customers and their people.

Built to last

No throwaway scripts dressed up as strategy. Solutions designed to hold up as you grow, and to compound as they run. The methodology is public: AN2B Labs publishes pre-registered experiments, negative results included.

I walk alongside you as your consultant so your business does the best AI work it can, building each solution with the best technology available for that job.
