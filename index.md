The Principles

## How the work
gets done.

### Kingdom first

This work is a calling before it is a business. I build for the Kingdom first, which shows up as integrity in the build, honesty in the room, and technology put in service of the people it touches.

BB4C

### Breath before code

Pause to understand the problem before writing the solution. The best system is the one built on the right diagnosis, not the fastest demo.

TWB

### Trust. Walk. Build.

In that order. Trust before the plan, walk before the sprint, build only what the first two have earned. It is how covenants work, and how good systems get made.

### You own your data

Your data is yours, fully portable, and never held hostage. I build on systems you control, so you are never locked into a tool you cannot walk away from.

### Intentions matter

A tool is only as good as the intentions behind it. I build for operators who want to do right by their customers and their people.

### Built to last

No throwaway scripts dressed up as strategy. Solutions designed to hold up as you grow, and to compound as they run. The methodology is public: [AN2B Labs](https://an2b.com/labs) publishes pre-registered experiments, negative results included.

I walk alongside you as your consultant so your business does the best AI work it can, building each solution with the best technology available for that job.

## Your 45-day business
transformation blueprint.

Turn a broad ambition into a practical plan. Together we map how work happens today, identify the constraints, and prioritize what to improve.

For CEOs, entrepreneurs, business owners and operations leaders who want to understand where AI could help before committing to implementation. Start with a recurring workflow, available information and the people responsible for reviewing the result.

### Understand the operation

Map workflows, handoffs, tools, and the people responsible. Record what is supported by evidence and what still needs checking.

### Choose the first opportunities

Evaluate candidate workflows, data readiness, permissions, and human review. Agree how a useful result would be measured.

### Prepare the blueprint

A prioritized roadmap, proposed implementation scope, responsibilities, and decisions for your team. Implementation is scoped separately.

The 45-day engagement begins at an agreed kickoff. Deliverables, participation, fees, and timing are confirmed in writing before paid work starts. The initial Norman interview is a separate discovery step.
